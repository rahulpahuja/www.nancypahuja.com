Nancy Pahuja Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Web Application PRD & Branding Kit

Project: Nancy Pahuja
Target: Women 25-55, South Asian Apparel (Lawn Suits)
Brand: Premium, Elegant, Artisanal
Colors: White (#FFFFFF), Graceful Blush (#F8D7DA), Rose Blush (#D98891), Deep Berry (#4A3B3D)
Typography: Cormorant Garamond (Serif), Montserrat (Sans-Serif)

Key Requirements:
- Google Sign-In exclusive authentication.
- Strict "No Returns, No Cancellation" policy enforcement.
- Product Management System (PMS) and Order Management System (OMS) for admins.
- Omnichannel notifications (WhatsApp/SMS).

Proposed Screen List:
1. Homepage: Elegant storytelling, hero collections, and artisanal highlights.
2. Product Listing Page (PLP): High-resolution grid with filters.
3. Product Detail Page (PDP): Gallery, size charts, and "Final Sale" badge.
4. Cart & Checkout: Policy acknowledgement and WhatsApp/SMS preference collection.
5. User Profile/Orders: Google-authenticated view of order history (no cancel button).
6. Admin Dashboard: Overview of sales and fulfillment status.
7. Admin PMS: Catalog management (Add/Edit products).
8. Admin OMS: Order grid and fulfillment pipeline management.
